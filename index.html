What's new

  • 1.7 Every row is now drawn at the same size, and you can choose how many bars go in each row.

  • 1.5 Overlapping rows are joined and re-cut at bar lines. Tuning and capo on the PDF. Sharper output. Highlights now recolour instead of repaint. Fixed missing pages and darkened frames.

  • 1.2 Now called folio, with a new look and logo. A library keeps every capture.
